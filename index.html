01. About Me

I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at BRAC University in 2026 and am a certified Full Stack Developer. I enjoy turning ideas into working systems from Laravel and MERN web apps to Solidity smart contracts, OpenGL games, and microcontroller-driven robots.

My undergraduate thesis explored how vehicles can detect road faults with computer vision, verify them with vision-language models, learn collaboratively through federated learning, and record trustworthy evidence on a permissioned blockchain.

I'm passionate about integrating AI/ML, cybersecurity and blockchain into practical applications that solve real problems.

Undergraduate Thesis · BRAC University · 2025–2026

A system where vehicles detect potholes, cracks and missing road signs from dashcam video, verify each detection with a vision-language model, improve the detection model together through federated learning without sharing raw data, and record reports and rewards on a permissioned blockchain.
